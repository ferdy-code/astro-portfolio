## The problem

Early-stage teams don't suffer from a lack of tools — they suffer from the tax of maintaining them. Planning live in ten places, so the plan was always stale.

## The approach

Flowboard treats the plan as the source of truth and AI as a collaborative editor. Rough notes become structured epics, estimates, and dependencies — with the human always in the loop.

- Natural-language planning that compiles to a real data model
- Board, timeline, and list views over the same underlying graph
- Sub-second search across every project, powered by Postgres full-text

## The stack

Astro and TypeScript on the front, PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security underneath, and a task queue orchestrating LLM calls so the interface never waits on the model.

## The outcome

Teams cut weekly planning overhead from hours to minutes. Flowboard reached 3,000 active workspaces in its first year with a conversion rate twice the category average.
