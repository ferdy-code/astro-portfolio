## The problem

Most invoicing tools treat Indonesian tax compliance as an afterthought: bolted-on currency formatting, no NPWP fields, no path to a proper Faktur Pajak. Freelancers end up patching spreadsheets together instead.

## The approach

InvoiceKit is Rupiah-native from the first schema. NPWP and Faktur Pajak (Coretax) fields are part of the data model from day one.

## The stack

Next.js and TypeScript on the front, PostgreSQL with Prisma underneath.

## Status

In planning. The PRD and data model are underway.
