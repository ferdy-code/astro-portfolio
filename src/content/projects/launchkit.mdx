## The problem

Most SaaS projects die before launch — not from a hard product problem, but from three weeks of wiring auth, billing, and marketing pages that every product needs anyway.

## The approach

LaunchKit packages the unglamorous 80% into a foundation that's genuinely production-grade: not a demo, but the actual architecture I ship for client work.

- Auth, teams, and entitlements with Stripe billing fully wired
- Marketing site, pricing, and docs sharing one design system
- Lighthouse-green defaults: static-first, near-zero JavaScript

## The stack

Astro for the static-first marketing surface, Node.js for the app layer, Stripe for billing and webhooks, and Tailwind CSS carrying a single design system across every page.

## The outcome

250+ developers have shipped with LaunchKit, several reaching their first $1k MRR within weeks — proof that boring, well-built foundations are a feature.
