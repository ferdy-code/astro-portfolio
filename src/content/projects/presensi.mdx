## The problem

Attendance tracking sounds simple until it has to work on real devices, in real conditions — flaky push delivery, auth edge cases, and Android version quirks that never show up in an emulator.

## The approach

A Flutter client talks to a Node/Express backend over JWT-authenticated APIs, with push notifications delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Getting it reliable meant real-device debugging, including platform-specific quirks down to Android 12.

## The stack

Flutter on the client, Node.js and Express on the backend, PostgreSQL for storage, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push.

## Status

Built and in active debugging and polish.
