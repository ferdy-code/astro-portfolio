## The problem

Product teams waited days for analytics questions to be answered by data teams. By the time the report arrived, the decision had already been made — often without data.

## The approach

Pulse puts self-serve exploration first: a query builder that compiles to optimized ClickHouse SQL, streaming results over WebSockets the moment they land.

- Funnel, retention, and cohort views with zero configuration
- Live dashboards that update as events stream in
- Saved questions that become the team's shared vocabulary

## The stack

React for the interactive canvas, Node.js for the streaming gateway, and ClickHouse doing the heavy lifting — handling billions of events with sub-second aggregation.

## The outcome

Time-to-insight dropped from days to seconds for 200+ product teams, and "ask the data team" disappeared from the weekly ritual entirely.
